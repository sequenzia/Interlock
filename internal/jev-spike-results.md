## Interpretation and decisions, written by hand after run 1 (Oct 16)

A follow-up probe ran after the main spike, 3 repeats per case, passing rule
only, to test two ideas before changing `facts.py`:

| Case | Baseline | + `lane_position` label | + `lane_right_of_lane_after` | + both |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| gap_violation, LANE_LEFT 3 to 2, expect ok | 0.48 | 0.47 | | |
| passing_pass, LANE_LEFT 2 to 1 with a slower vehicle ahead, expect ok | 0.49 | 0.54 | 0.48 | 0.56 |
| passing_cruise, IDLE in lane 1 with nobody slower, expect violation | 0.69 | 0.72 | 0.68 | 0.71 |

The model id probe showed `jev-1.13.0` is accepted as a request model and
reports itself; `jev-latest` resolves to the same version today.

Decisions:

1. Pin `jev-1.13.0` as `DEFAULT_MODEL` in `backend/jev_client.py`.
2. Shape B stays. Shape A was faster (143 versus 197 ms per step) and 29%
   cheaper in tokens, but disagreed with B by 0.32 on the LANE_LEFT gap
   question, and the per-candidate cache needs one state per candidate.
3. JSON state stays. Prose was within noise (mean diff 0.04) and no cheaper.
4. The regex comparison facts from plan 5.4 are not built. Speed comparisons
   are crisp: 0.03 to 0.04 at or below the limit, 0.93 to 0.94 above. Gap
   comparisons have the right sign at every probe but are soft (9 m gives
   0.53 as typed). That softness tracks the negation in the rule text, not
   the arithmetic: the positive rephrase gives 0.91 at 9 m.
5. The UI will offer a positive rephrase for rules that read as "No ... if
   ..." and keep the operator's text. It never overrides.
6. The passing rule is genuinely fuzzy for Jev: about 0.5 on lane-left moves
   and about 0.7 on cruising in the left lane. Positive labels and the
   lane-to-the-right block do not move it, so `facts.py` is unchanged. With
   T_ALLOW 0.2 and T_BLOCK 0.8 those steps escalate, which is the designed
   behavior for a fuzzy rule and the human-on-the-loop moment of the demo.
   Expect this rule to dominate the escalation rate; Day 5 measures it.
7. Stability std is at most 0.05 over identical requests, so thresholds at
   0.2 and 0.8 leave a wide margin and decisions repeat for the same facts
   even before caching.
8. Latency per step at shape B: p50 157 ms, p95 247 ms. Cost about $0.011 per
   60-step episode. Playback at 2 steps per second is comfortable.
9. From the first live shielded episode (seed 0, zone dropped at step 10):
   while the zone was ahead and in the facts, Jev scored the zone rule 0.04
   to 0.05 for staying out and 0.84 for entering. After the ego passed the
   zone and the facts carried no zone, the rule text alone kept p at 0.31 to
   0.66 for any action in lane 3. Jev weights a rule that asserts a closure
   over empty facts. Consequence: a zone and its rule share a lifecycle in
   `ConstraintStore`; dropping a zone arms the rule, passing the last zone
   disables it, and the CLI's standing rules exclude the zone rule.
